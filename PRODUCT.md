# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

International rice importers, distributors, private-label buyers and merchant
exporters evaluating an Indian mill before requesting a commercial quotation.
They may browse on desktop while reviewing specifications or on mobile while
coordinating samples, documents and WhatsApp follow-up.

## Product Purpose

Present UrbanFresh Rice Mills as a credible, specification-led sourcing partner
and help a serious buyer move from mill verification to one complete,
reviewable international RFQ. Success means a qualified buying brief, not
consumer catalogue browsing or inflated traffic.

## Brand Personality

Grounded, exacting and hospitable. The site should feel like a well-run mill
buyer desk: calm enough for due diligence, specific enough for procurement and
warm enough to invite a direct conversation.

## Anti-references

- Generic rice-trader catalogues that substitute decorative product claims for
  verifiable mill evidence.
- Consumer ecommerce, domestic-delivery framing and aggressive discount-led
  sales design.
- AI landing-page clichés: unsupported metrics, endless identical cards,
  glassmorphism, gradient text and decorative compliance claims.
- Dense export jargon, tiny controls or desktop layouts that are merely scaled
  down on mobile.

## Design Principles

1. Show the physical mill and the evidence path before making assurances.
2. Keep the buyer's next decision visible, especially the RFQ and WhatsApp
   continuation paths.
3. Use specification structure to create confidence without inventing facts.
4. Preserve calm visual rhythm, clear reading widths and decisive hierarchy.
5. Adapt navigation, tables, forms and calls to action to the user's actual
   screen rather than allowing horizontal overflow.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA for contrast, keyboard access, focus visibility, semantics
and labelled forms. Support 320 px mobile screens through large desktops,
44-by-44-pixel touch targets, text zoom, reduced motion and safe-area insets.
