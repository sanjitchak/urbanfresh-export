# UrbanFresh Export agent instructions

## Start every chat here

Read `CHANGELOG.md` and `CONTENT-VERIFICATION.md` completely before inspecting
or changing this repository.

## Truth and launch gate

- Never invent export history, capacity, specifications, certifications, lab
  names, residue limits, packing configurations, MOQ, lead time, ports or
  commercial terms.
- Treat the existing `sanjitchak/urbanfresh` repository as the factual source.
- Do not add a named lab, port, container payload, MOQ or lead time unless that
  source is updated with the fact.
- Do not submit a sitemap until DNS, HTTPS and Search Console access are
  verified.
- The `.com` site is for international buyers. Do not copy domestic pricing,
  city-delivery framing or the 29-page `.in` catalogue.

## Keep the handoff current

Update `CHANGELOG.md` whenever material work is completed, blocked, deployed or
externally verified. Keep entries newest first and record validation and open
items without secrets.

## Generated pages

Root HTML, `robots.txt` and `sitemap.xml` are generated by
`scripts/build_site.py`. Edit the generator, then rebuild. Do not hand-edit
generated pages.
